# Cinema Ticket Sales Prediction using Regression Models

## What is the objective of the project?
In this project, the company is trying to decide whether sales revenue is good enough with respect to the other situations they face in the Cinema hall Industry. We are here to help them make a data-driven decision.

## What is the data in the project?
In this project, we work with a dataset [available on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/cinema-ticket/data). The data includes information about the sale history of movies in cinema halls. The granularity is shown in time. It means the total sales computed by date, cinema, movie, and show time. Also, the occupation per cent (on available cinema capacity) is computed at show time. Some tickets might be cancelled due to a reservation or any other reason. Some features are provided below:
- film_code: Unique movie id.
- cinema_code: Unique cinema id.
- total_sales: Total sale per screening time.
- ticket_price: Price of ticket at show time.
- tickets_sold: Number of tickets solds.
